# Northstar Demo Company — Employee Handbook

This is fictional sample data for development and testing.

## Annual leave

Employees receive 22 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave requests must be submitted to the employee's manager at least five working days in advance.

## Remote work

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week with manager approval. Customer records must only be accessed through company-approved devices and the company VPN.

## Equipment expenses

Equipment purchases above 150 GBP require written manager approval before purchase. Receipts must be submitted within 30 days.
